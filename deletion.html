Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of information stored by RAEES SYSTEMS.

Reels Multi Manager

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · Applies to Reels Multi Manager / RAEES SYSTEMS

How to request deletion

If you connected an Instagram professional account to Reels Multi Manager and want the information held by RAEES SYSTEMS deleted, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to empireya.marketing@gmail.com with the subject RAEES SYSTEMS Data Deletion.
  2. Include your Instagram username and a way for our administrator to verify that you are authorized to make the request.
  3. Specify whether you want the connected account and its stored related content removed, or request information about the records we hold.
  4. Our team will verify the request, process eligible deletion and reply with its outcome, subject to applicable legal and security obligations.

Never include your Instagram password, login codes or OAuth tokens in your deletion request.

Revoke Instagram authorization

You can separately revoke RAEES SYSTEMS access using Instagram's settings for connected apps and websites. Revoking access stops future authorized access but may not automatically delete information already stored by our application. Use the email request above to ask us to delete stored information.

What data may be affected

Depending on your use, deletion may include connected Instagram identifiers, stored access tokens, media uploaded to the service, scheduled jobs, captions and related account records. Some audit information and backups may require separate retention where necessary to meet legal and security obligations.
