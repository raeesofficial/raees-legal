Privacy Policy

How RAEES SYSTEMS handles Instagram account and service data.

Reels Multi Manager

Effective date: 2 November 2026. Service: Reels Multi Manager (RAEES SYSTEMS).

1. About this service

RAEES SYSTEMS provides tools for authorized administrators to connect and manage Instagram professional accounts, organize media, schedule publishing, and review available performance information. This policy describes data handling for this application.

2. Information we process

  • Instagram account identifiers, usernames and available profile information received through authorized Meta APIs.
  • OAuth access tokens and permission-related information needed to provide a connection.
  • Uploaded videos, cover images, captions, schedules, publishing results and available analytics.
  • Administrative login sessions and technical logs necessary for operation, security, and troubleshooting.

Instagram passwords and email verification codes are entered on the official Instagram login experience, not collected by RAEES SYSTEMS.

3. How information is used

We process information to connect accounts with their authorization, manage selected media, perform requested publishing actions, show status and analytics, diagnose errors, and protect the service. Additional features such as comment management are only used when enabled and authorized.

4. Access and sharing

Access is limited to authorized service administrators and technical service providers required for operation. Requests to Instagram are sent through Meta's official APIs in accordance with the permissions granted. Access tokens must not be publicly disclosed. We do not sell Instagram access tokens.

5. Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards to protect connected-account information. OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted form in the application's database. We retain information while required to provide the connected service or for legitimate operational and legal purposes. Deletion requests are handled as described in our Data Deletion Instructions. Removal of data from backups may follow applicable backup-retention procedures.

6. Your choices

You may remove the application's permissions through Instagram's connected apps and websites settings. To request deletion of information held by RAEES SYSTEMS, follow the data deletion steps. Removing authorization may prevent connected features from functioning.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to empireya.marketing@gmail.com.
